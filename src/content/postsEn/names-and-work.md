My Chinese name is 曹益波. I use Kateiso Cao as my English display name, Yibo Cao as the romanized form of my name, and Kateio as another name. On this website, all four refer to me.

My personal website is [kateiso.dev](https://kateiso.dev/), and my GitHub account is [Kateiso](https://github.com/Kateiso). These linked profiles help distinguish my work from that of people with similar names.

## My work

I explore AI, XR and everyday life, with an interest in spatial experiences and the practical division of responsibility between a person and a system.

- [AI into Dining: Wangge Yucun](/en/projects/ai-into-dining.html) connects brand design, meal packages, short-video workflows and customer operations.
- [Human–AI Co-Memory](/en/blog/posts/human-ai-co-memory.html) explores how people and AI retain and revise the context of collaboration.
- [Creative workflow tools](/en/blog/posts/wiring-mcp-and-chrome-devtools-for-creative-workflows.html) records existing experiments with MCP, browser tools and small automations.
